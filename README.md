# hr-analytics-portfolio
HR Analytics portfolio showcasing Excel data cleaning, attendance analysis, Pivot Tables, KPI reporting, and dashboard development.
## Project Overview
This project demonstrates practical data analysis and reporting skills through a simulated Human Resources dataset.

The objective is to assess employee data quality, analyse workforce composition, and investigate attendance patterns to support informed HR decision-making.

## Business Questions
- How many employees are recorded in the dataset?
- What data-quality issues could affect HR reporting?
- How is the workforce distributed by department and status?
- What attendance patterns are present in the records?
- What limitations should HR stakeholders consider?

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Excel formulas and data cleaning
- Pivotcharts
- Data-quality auditing
- KPI reporting and business interpretation

## Work Completed
- Identified and reviewed duplicate employee records.
- Flagged missing department information.
- Standardised inconsistent employment-status values.
- Calculated workforce metrics and salary summaries.
- Analysed attendance status across 1,200 records.
- Documented data-quality findings and limitations.

## Key Finding
The attendance dataset contains 1,200 records.
The department summary accounts for 1,188 records, leaving 12 records without a department classification.
These records require investigation before department-level attendance results can be considered complete.

## Data Limitations
Missing department information can affect comparisons between departments. Findings should be interpreted with these limitations in mind.

- Data-quality audit
- Workforce and attendance summaries
